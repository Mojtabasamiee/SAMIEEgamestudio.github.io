YOUR LOGO GOES HERE

1) Put your own logo image in this folder.
2) Name the file exactly: logo.png
3) The website header will detect and display it automatically.
4) Recommended: transparent PNG, landscape or square, with a clear version for a dark background.
5) If your file is JPG or WebP, either export it as PNG or update the logo.png path in every HTML page.

No logo artwork is included because the studio will provide its own logo.
